Grid Manager

Keyboard: on the map, arrow keys move between squares, Enter or Space builds the chosen machine (or removes it if it's already there), and Delete removes whatever is on the square. Number keys choose what to build, R chooses Remove, Ctrl+Z undoes. On the chart, left and right arrows choose an hour. Flexible jobs on the timeline can be focused with Tab and moved with the left and right arrows.
