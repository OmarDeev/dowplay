## How to play Neon Snake

Guide the snake around the board and eat the glowing pink orbs. Every orb makes you one segment longer — and a little faster. The game ends if you hit a wall or bite your own tail, so plan your turns ahead.

## Tips

- Hug the edges early on to keep the middle of the board open.
- Don't chase an orb into a corner unless you have a way out.
- Your best score is saved in your browser, so you can keep beating it.
