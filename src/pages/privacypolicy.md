## Information We Collect

### Information You give Us

We do not require You to create an account to use our games. If You contact Us by email or through the contact form on the Website, We receive Your name, Your email address and the content of Your message. We use this information only to reply to You.

### Information collected automatically

When You use the Service, some information is collected automatically, by Us or by our Service Providers, such as:

- Your Device's IP address, Device type and model, operating system and its version, browser type, language and approximate location (country or city level, derived from the IP address)
- Unique Device identifiers, including the **advertising identifier** of Your mobile Device (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS when You allow tracking)
- How You use the Service, for example the pages You visit, the games You play, the time spent in them, and crash and performance data

### Cookies and similar technologies

- **Website:** with Your consent, the Website uses cookies from Google Analytics to measure how visitors use it (see "Analytics" below). You can accept or decline these cookies in the banner shown on Your first visit, and change Your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.
- **Web games:** our web games may save Your progress, settings and best scores in Your browser's local storage. This data stays on Your Device and is not sent to Us.
- **Applications:** the advertising and analytics tools built into our Applications use Device identifiers and similar technologies as described below.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- Provide, operate and maintain the Service
- Show advertisements in our Applications, which keeps our games free
- Process and restore in-app purchases
- Understand how the Service is used, so We can fix bugs and improve our games
- Reply to Your messages and support requests
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security issues
- Comply with legal obligations

## Advertising

Some of our Applications show advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** and, in some Applications, by other advertising networks. These networks may collect and use Your Device's advertising identifier, IP address, Device information and information about Your interaction with ads in order to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Depending on Your choices and Your location, ads may be personalized to Your interests or non-personalized.

- **Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland:** where required by law, our Applications ask for Your consent before personalized ads are shown or Your data is used for advertising purposes. You can change Your choice from within the Application where this option is offered.
- **Android:** You can reset Your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **iOS:** our Applications only use Your IDFA if You allow it in the "Allow Apps to Request to Track" prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## In-App Purchases

Some of our Applications offer optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive or store Your payment card details. We only receive the information needed to confirm and restore Your purchase, such as a transaction identifier and the product purchased.

## Analytics

The Website uses **Google Analytics 4**. If You accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information such as the pages You visit, the games You start, Your browser, Device type and approximate location, and stores it in cookies on Your Device. If You decline, no analytics cookies are set. Google may still receive limited, cookie-less signals that are not used to identify You. Some of our Applications may also use analytics and crash-reporting tools to help Us understand usage and fix problems.

You can learn more about how Google handles this data at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- Access the Personal Data We hold about You

To exercise these rights, contact Us using the details below. ## Children's Privacy

**Website:** the web games on the Website are made for students aged about 12 to 18, and they can be played without giving Us any Personal Data. There are no accounts, no chat and no advertising on the Website, and the games save progress only in the browser on Your Device. Analytics cookies are only set if the visitor accepts them in the cookie banner. The contact form is meant for teachers, parents and other adults; We ask children not to send Us Personal Data.

**Applications:** our mobile Applications are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under the age of 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of their country. If You are a parent or guardian and You believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us and We will delete it. If We need to rely on consent as a legal basis for processing Your information and Your country requires consent from a parent, We may require Your parent's consent before We collect and use that information.
